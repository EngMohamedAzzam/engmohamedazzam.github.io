09 / MASTER'S THESIS

Sustainable Financing of Water Supply

Case study: the Red Sea Governorate for 2030 vision · M.Sc. Water Engineering · TU Berlin

The question

How can water-supply capacity, infrastructure investment and financing be planned together to support expected development through 2030?

Method

  1. Project population and water demand to 2030 using demographic and activity-based assumptions.
  2. Assess domestic, agricultural and tourism-related demand.
  3. Develop water-supply network cost models and compare technical/cost alternatives.
  4. Evaluate desalination investment requirements and lifecycle costs.
  5. Analyse financing sources, water pricing, tariffs and cost-recovery pathways.
  6. Translate the analysis into an investment decision-support perspective.
